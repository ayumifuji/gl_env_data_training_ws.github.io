# Great Lakes Environmental Data Training Workshop

## Workshop Summary
Hosted by the Cooperative Institute for Great Lakes Research (CIGLR) in collaboration with the School for Environment and Sustainability (SEAS) and the Michigan Institute for Data and AI in Society (MIDAS) at the University of Michigan, the Great Lake Environmental Data Training Workshop was held at the Central Campus Classroom Building, University of Michigan on June 5, 2026. This workshop is supported by the U.S. National Science Foundation under Award No. 2319044. Any opinions, findings, and conclusions or recommendations expressed are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the views of NSF.
